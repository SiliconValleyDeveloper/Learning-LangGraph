# Contoso Ops — Employee Handbook

Contoso Ops runs internal AI copilots for SRE and support teams.
Default stack: Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, Ollama, pgvector.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are **10:00–18:00 IST** on weekdays.
On-call engineers follow the weekend rotation listed in `#ops-oncall`.

## Leave policy

- Annual leave: 24 days
- Sick leave: 12 days
- Manager approval required for leave longer than 3 consecutive days
- Ticket code for leave requests: **HR-LEAVE-24**

## Support channels

| Need | Channel |
|------|---------|
| Product questions | #product-help |
| Infrastructure incidents | #ops-oncall |
| AI tooling | #langgraph-lab |
| Access / SSO issues | #iam-help |

## Knowledge policy

Public runbooks and FAQs may be indexed for RAG. Documents marked
`visibility=confidential` must never leave the company network.
Retrieval systems must cite sources and refuse when evidence is weak.
