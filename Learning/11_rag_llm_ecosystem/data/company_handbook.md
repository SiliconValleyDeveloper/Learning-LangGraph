# Company Handbook

Acme Learning Labs builds internal AI copilots for engineering teams.
Our default stack is Python, FastAPI, Angular, Ollama, LangChain, and LangGraph.

## Working hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00–16:00 IST on weekdays. Async updates are
welcome outside that window.

## Support channels

- Product questions: #product-help
- Infrastructure incidents: #ops-oncall
- AI tooling: #langgraph-lab

## Knowledge policy

Public documentation may be indexed for RAG demos. Private documents under
`data/private/` contain employee or customer details and must never be committed
to git. Retrieval may still use them locally when the private folder is present.
