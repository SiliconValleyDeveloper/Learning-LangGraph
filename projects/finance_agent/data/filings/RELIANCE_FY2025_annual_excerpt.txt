1. Management discussion — capital allocation
--------------------------------------------
The Board continues to prioritise disciplined capital allocation across oil-to-chemicals,
retail, and digital services. Net debt reduced during the year as free cash flow from
the energy business funded growth capex in retail and Jio platforms. Management reiterated
that leverage will remain within the Board-approved band and that surplus cash after
maintenance capex may support shareholder returns.

2. Debt and liquidity
---------------------
Gross debt declined modestly year-on-year. Cash and cash equivalents rose, supported by
operating cash flows. The company maintains undrawn bank lines and a diversified maturity
profile. Interest coverage remained comfortable. Currency exposure on overseas borrowings
is partially hedged. Contingent liabilities related to tax and regulatory matters are
disclosed in the notes; no material new contingent liabilities were recognised in FY2025.

3. Segment commentary — energy
------------------------------
Refining margins normalised versus the prior peak cycle. Petrochemical volumes improved
on domestic demand recovery. Exploration & production contribution remained stable. The
company highlighted progress on new energy initiatives including solar manufacturing and
battery storage pilots, which remain early-stage relative to core O2C cash generation.

4. Segment commentary — retail and digital
------------------------------------------
Retail footprint expansion continued with higher same-store sales in grocery and fashion.
Jio subscriber growth and ARPU trends supported digital EBITDA. Management stated that
integration of merchant partnerships and fintech pilots will be measured against unit
economics rather than vanity metrics. Capex intensity in retail is expected to moderate
as store density in key cities approaches target coverage.

--------------------
